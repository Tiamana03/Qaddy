# Qaddy Product Specification
Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Last Updated: September 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines what Qaddy is, who it is for, and how every major feature should work.

It is the single source of truth for product functionality.

If the Founder Blueprint explains why Qaddy exists, this document explains what Qaddy delivers.

---

# Product Vision

Qaddy is the operating system for a golfer's life.

Rather than using multiple disconnected apps, golfers should be able to manage every part of their golfing journey from one beautifully designed platform.

---

# Target Audience

Primary Audience

- Golfers aged 25–45
- Regular social golfers
- Competitive golfers
- Golf travellers
- Members of golf clubs
- Weekend golfers

Secondary Audience

- New golfers
- Junior golfers
- Golf coaches
- Golf groups

# Feature Overview

## Dashboard

Purpose

The golfer's home screen.

Should immediately answer:

"What matters today?"

Displays:

- Upcoming rounds
- Recent scores
- Personal statistics
- Friends activity
- Challenges
- Quick actions

---

## Ask Qaddy

Purpose

The golfer's AI assistant.

Capabilities include:

- Golf rules
- Equipment advice
- Course strategy
- Handicap questions
- Practice suggestions
- General golf knowledge

Future versions may include personalised coaching.

---

## Golf IQ

Purpose

Help golfers improve.

Features include:

- Performance trends
- Strength analysis
- Weakness analysis
- Practice recommendations
- AI insights

---

## My Bag

Purpose

Manage golf equipment.

Includes:

- Clubs
- Distances
- Equipment notes
- Wedges
- Driver settings
- Favourite balls

Future

AI club recommendations.

---

Celebrate milestones.

## Profile

Purpose

The golfer's identity inside Qaddy.

Contains

- Statistics

## Premium

Purpose

Provide additional value for committed golfers.

Examples

- Advanced Golf IQ
- AI coaching
- Premium statistics
- Exclusive badges
- Future features

Premium should enhance the experience, never restrict the core experience.

---

# Product Principles

Every feature should:

- Help golfers enjoy golf more.
- Be intuitive.
- Feel premium.
- Load quickly.
- Respect user privacy.
- Work consistently across devices.

---

# Definition of Success

Qaddy succeeds when golfers:

- Open the app regularly.
- Record their rounds.
- Improve their game.
- Invite friends.
- Plan golf trips.
- Build lasting memories.

---

# Final Statement

Qaddy is not simply another golf application.

It is the digital home for every golfer.

Every feature should reinforce that purpose.
